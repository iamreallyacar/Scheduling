# Business Situation and Problem Statement

Status: DRAFT. Fictional scenario for a rebuild of the Scheduling project. Figures are placeholders to confirm.

## 1. Business situation

**Company:** a mid-sized tire manufacturer with one plant, about 15-25 machines and 3 shifts. It makes roughly 40 tire models for OEM and replacement-market customers.

**Production process:** each tire passes through stages such as extrusion, building, molding/curing and inspection. Each stage runs on a specific machine type (for example extrusion line, building machine, molding press).

**Current way of working:**
- Planners build the weekly schedule in spreadsheets and on a whiteboard.
- Supervisors give planners machine status and maintenance windows verbally or by message.
- Sales promises due dates by checking with the planner, so the answer depends on who is asked.
- Customers have started penalizing late deliveries, and rush orders arrive weekly.

## 2. Problem statement

Production planners cannot see in one place which orders are at risk, which machines are available, and what a new or rush order would displace. The schedule is manual and goes stale within hours. Machine breakdowns and maintenance windows are not reflected in it.

**Consequences:**
- Orders ship late and are found to be late only when the due date is near.
- Machines sit idle while others are overloaded.
- Due-date promises to customers are unreliable.
- Re-planning after a disruption takes hours.

## 3. Who is affected

| Stakeholder | Impact |
|---|---|
| Production planners (primary users) | Spend their time rebuilding schedules by hand |
| Shift supervisors | Work from outdated schedules |
| Sales and customer service | Cannot give reliable due dates |
| Plant manager | Has no view of delivery risk or machine use |

## 4. Candidate success measures

Placeholders. Baselines and targets to be set with stakeholders.

- On-time delivery rate
- Machine utilization
- Time to re-plan after a disruption
- Share of due-date promises met

## 5. Focus

The rebuild focuses on the **production planner**. Other stakeholders are secondary for now.
